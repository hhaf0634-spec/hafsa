The Schedule

Day-by-Day Bespoke Itinerary

Arrive in Naples where your private driver welcomes you at the baggage carousel. Board your executive Mercedes S-Class for the scenic climb along the cliff-hugging Costiera Amalfitana. Check into your deluxe sea-view sanctuary at Le Sirenuse. Enjoy evening welcome cocktails and an inaugural sunset canapé reception overlooking the vertical lights of Positano.

Private VIP Airport Greeter Le Sirenuse Positano Sunset Cocktail Reception

Step aboard your privately chartered luxury yacht at Positano pier. Sail west toward Capri, threading through the iconic Faraglioni arches. Drop anchor in secluded crystalline coves for paddleboarding and swimming. Enjoy a chilled prosecco and lobster lunch served on board, followed by tender docking at Marina Grande for an afternoon stroll through Capri's designer boutiques and the Gardens of Augustus.

Full-Day Yacht Charter Champagne Lunch On Deck Capri Shore Excursion

Ascend into the ethereal heights of Ravello. Tour the historic grounds of Villa Rufolo and Villa Cimbrone's dramatic Infinity Terrace, towering 1,200 feet above the coast. Later, enter a private family-run lemon farm for an exclusive cooking masterclass, crafting fresh handmade tagliolini with Amalfi sfusato lemons and paired local DOC white wines.

Villa Cimbrone VIP Pass Private Cooking Masterclass

Trek the legendary Sentiero degli Dei with your private alpine certified guide, enjoying panoramic cliffside views across the entire Sorrento Peninsula. Finish in Nocelle for an artisanal limoncello cellar tour and farm-to-table lunch under ancient vine trellises.

Discover the maritime republic heritage of Amalfi town with private access to the Cathedral of St. Andrew and Cloister of Paradise. Next, sail into the geological spectacle of Fiordo di Furore. Conclude with a reserved 7-course tasting menu at Michelin-starred Ristorante Quattro Passi.

Relax at the exclusive La Fontelina or One Fire Beach Club with reserved premium front-row sunbeds. Indulge in an afternoon thermal spa treatment at Monastero Santa Rosa, followed by a lavish private farewell gala dinner on the terrace.

Enjoy a final unhurried terrace breakfast overlooking the morning sea mist. Your private Mercedes chauffeur escorts you smoothly back to Naples Capodichino Airport or onward train connections.
